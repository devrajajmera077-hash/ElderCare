# ElderCare — Senior Citizen Care & Coordination

A Hindi-first digital platform designed to make everyday support easier for senior citizens, with an initial focus on Rajasthan.

## Problem

Senior citizens may face difficulties managing daily reminders, accessing reliable welfare information, and coordinating routine support with family members. Complex digital interfaces can make these tasks harder.

## Proposed Solution

ElderCare aims to provide a simple and accessible platform for senior citizen support and caregiver coordination.

## Planned Features

- Medicine and appointment reminders
- Daily well-being check-ins
- Consent-based caregiver coordination
- Trusted emergency contact options
- Verified government welfare information
- Accessible interface with large buttons and simple language

## Proposed Technology Stack

- Frontend: Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
- Backend and database: Supabase

## Privacy and Safety

ElderCare is intended to complement existing healthcare and emergency services. It will not diagnose medical conditions or prescribe treatment. Reminders do not confirm medicine intake, and emergency assistance will not be claimed unless verified.
